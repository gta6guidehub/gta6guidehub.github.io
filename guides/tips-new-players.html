15 Tips for New Players in GTA 6

Fifteen things experienced players wish they had known on day one in Leonida. Short, practical, and free of spoilers beyond the early game.

  1. Finish the opening missions first. They unlock the map, safehouses and contacts everything else depends on.
  2. Bank your cash. Money in the bank is safe; money in your pocket can be lost.
  3. Buy a good all-rounder before a supercar. A $98,500 Dominator GTX beats a $1.78 million garage queen you cannot afford to upgrade.
  4. Learn the escape, not just the chase. Break line of sight, change cars, use alleys — fighting the police is usually the expensive option.
  5. Switch characters often. Lucia and Jason have different contacts, money and side activities. Check both phones.
  6. Mark the map. Ammu-Nation, mod shops, safehouses and hospitals — knowing where they are saves you in a crisis.
  7. Save before big missions. Use safehouses and quick-save. A failed heist costs time; an unsaved one costs progress.
  8. Upgrade handling before top speed. Brakes, tyres and suspension win more races than a bigger engine.
  9. Collect from businesses regularly. Uncollected income is wasted income.
  10. Try every side activity once. Each one unlocks something — a contact, a discount, a story hint.
  11. Keep a cheap escape car. Park something plain near your safehouse. Flashy cars attract attention.
  12. Watch the weather. Rain changes braking distances a lot on Vice City's streets. Slow down before the corner, not in it.
  13. Change your settings. Turn on subtitles, adjust the driving camera, and set the minimap zoom to your taste on day one.
  14. Explore the Keys and the interior. The best driving roads and several collectibles are far from downtown.
  15. Use the trackers on this site. Our mission checklist and completion tracker keep your progress visible — and free, in your own browser.

Most of all: take your time. Leonida is a big state, and the best moments in GTA 6 are the ones you stumble into between missions.

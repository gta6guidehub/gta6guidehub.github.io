Lucia and Jason — Character Guide for GTA 6

GTA 6 stars two protagonists for the first time in the series' modern era: Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval. Their partnership — and their differences — shape the whole game. Here is what we know from the story so far, and how to play each of them well.

Lucia Caminos

Lucia is the driving force of the story. Fresh out of Leonida Penitentiary at the start of the game, she is determined never to go back — and willing to do almost anything to build a life on her own terms. She is sharp, decisive and quick to read people, which makes her the natural planner of the pair's bigger scores. In gameplay terms, Lucia's contacts lean toward the Vice City underworld: fixers, fences and people who know where the money moves.

Jason Duval

Jason is a Leonida local who wants a quiet life — and keeps getting pulled back into trouble. He grew up around the state's rougher edges, knows the back roads and the swamp towns, and has the practical skills: driving, mechanics, and staying calm when a plan falls apart. His contacts are more local and blue-collar: tow yards, garages, and people in Port Gellhorn and the Keys.

How switching works

You can switch between Lucia and Jason almost any time outside missions. Each keeps their own cash, cars, clothes and phone contacts, and each has unique side activities and random encounters. The game sometimes suggests a switch before a mission — take the hint, because that character usually has the right skills or contacts for the job.

Playing them well

  • Check both phones. New work, messages and map markers are character-specific.
  • Balance their money. A broke character cannot buy gear for their own missions.
  • Use Jason for driving-heavy work and Lucia for planning-heavy scores — generally, though both can do everything.
  • Do each character's side activities. They unlock story hints and unique rewards.

Their relationship

The story is, at its core, about two people trying to build something together in a state that keeps knocking them down. Missions swing between careful planning and desperate improvisation, and the best moments in the game come from how Lucia and Jason cover each other's weaknesses. Play both sides of the story — skipping one character's missions means missing half the game.

Best Vehicles in GTA 6 — Top Picks by Class

With dozens of vehicles in Leonida, the question is never "what is the fastest car?" — it is "what is the fastest car worth your money?" This guide ranks the standouts by class, using community-estimated prices and top speeds. All figures are player estimates, not official Rockstar data.

Best overall value: Vapid Dominator GTX

At around $98,500 with an estimated top speed of 118 mph, the Dominator GTX is the best first serious purchase in the game. It is a muscle car with strong acceleration, forgiving handling, and cheap upgrades. For new players, this is the car to buy before anything flashy.

Fastest in a straight line: Ocelot Pariah

The Pariah's estimated 136 mph top speed makes it the straight-line king of our comparison table, at roughly $420,000. It is less forgiving in corners and in the rain, so it suits highway runs and races more than tight Vice City streets.

Best supercar: Itali GTB Custom

At about $1,180,000 and an estimated 131 mph, the Itali GTB Custom is the dream purchase — but it is a late-game goal, not a first buy. Its 0–60 mph time of around 2.6 seconds is the quickest in our table, which matters more than top speed in most races.

Best SUV: Pfister Astron Custom

Off-road sections, swamp roads and the Leonida Keys are much easier in an SUV. The Astron Custom, around $168,000, handles rough ground, carries its speed well, and doubles as a practical daily driver for missions that need cargo space.

How to choose

  • New player: Dominator GTX or Vigero ZX — cheap, fast enough, easy to drive.
  • Racer: Pariah for straights, Itali GTB Custom for acceleration.
  • Explorer: Astron Custom SUV for the Keys, swamps and dirt roads.
  • Collector: Sultan RS Classic — expensive, but the most rewarding to drive well.

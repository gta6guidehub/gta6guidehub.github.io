GTA 6 Beginner's Guide — Start Smart in Leonida

Grand Theft Auto VI drops you into Leonida — a huge fictional Florida — with two protagonists, Lucia and Jason, and a whole state to learn. This beginner's guide covers the first ten hours: what to do first, what to ignore, and how to avoid the classic new-player mistakes.

First things first: play the story

The opening missions are more than a tutorial. They teach you the driving, shooting and switching mechanics, introduce Lucia and Jason, and unlock the map piece by piece. Rushing past them to free-roam sounds fun, but you will miss unlocks — safehouses, contacts and vehicle storage — that make everything else easier. Finish at least the first eight story missions before you start chasing side content seriously.

Learn to switch characters

Lucia and Jason have different strengths, contacts and side activities. Switching is instant when you are not mid-mission, and each character keeps their own money, wardrobe and vehicles. A good habit: switch whenever one character is low on cash or stuck far from your next objective. Some missions are character-specific, so check both characters' phones for new work.

Money: slow and steady wins

Early-game money comes from jobs, taxi work, deliveries and small side gigs — not from big heists. Heists pay well but need preparation, crew and upfront costs. Use our earnings calculator to set a realistic goal: a $98,500 muscle car is about 20 hours of steady work at $5,000 an hour, while a $1.78 million supercar is a long-term project. Bank your cash often; carrying huge sums makes you a target in free roam.

Driving and the wanted system

Leonida's police respond to what they can see. Breaking line of sight, changing vehicles and staying out of their vision cone drops your wanted level faster than simply outrunning them. Keep a cheap, inconspicuous car parked near your safehouse for escapes. And remember: the fastest car is not always the best getaway car — handling on wet Vice City streets matters more than top speed.

Five mistakes new players make

  • Spending everything on one car. Buy a solid all-rounder first, upgrade it, and save the supercar for later.
  • Ignoring the map. Ammu-Nation, mod shops, safehouses and hospitals are worth memorising — mark them.
  • Skipping side activities. They unlock contacts, discounts and story hints.
  • Never saving. Use safehouses and the quick-save option before risky missions.
  • Fighting every cop. A two-star wanted level is usually cheaper to escape than to fight.

Your first ten hours, in order

  1. Play the opening story missions until the map opens up.
  2. Buy a cheap reliable car and a basic weapon set.
  3. Try one side activity for each character.
  4. Save up for your first property or garage space.
  5. Then — and only then — start planning your first heist.

Leonida rewards patience. Learn the streets, build your cash slowly, and the big scores will come.

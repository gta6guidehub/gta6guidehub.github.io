Vice City Map and Key Locations in GTA 6

GTA 6 returns to Vice City — but Leonida is far bigger than the city. The map covers the neon streets of Vice Beach, the swamps of the interior, the island chain of the Leonida Keys, and small towns like Port Gellhorn. Here are the key locations and why each one matters.

Vice City: the heart of the map

Vice City is the dense urban core: Ocean Drive's hotels, the downtown skyline, the port, and the airport. Most early story missions happen here, and it holds the highest concentration of shops, mod garages and safehouses. Learn the main avenues first — the beachfront road, the highway loop and the bridge to the mainland — and you will rarely get lost.

Vice Beach and Ocean Drive

The beachfront is where the game's famous neon look lives. It is also a great early-game area for side activities and photo spots. Traffic is heavy at night, so use the beach road for cruising, not for escapes.

The Leonida Keys

South of the city, the Keys are a chain of small islands joined by bridges — quiet roads, water on both sides, and some of the best driving scenery in the game. They are also useful for losing a wanted level: few side streets means the police have fewer ways to cut you off, but also fewer places for you to hide.

Port Gellhorn

This faded coastal town on the west of the map is where several mid-game missions take place. It is smaller and rougher than Vice City, with cheap properties and a rundown charm. It is a good place to stash a second safehouse if you operate on that side of the state.

Mount Kalaga and the interior

The interior of Leonida is swamp, farmland and forest around Mount Kalaga. Roads are narrower, wildlife is a real hazard, and off-road vehicles earn their keep. Some collectibles and hunting-style side activities live out here — bring an SUV and a full tank.

Key locations checklist

  • Ammu-Nation — several branches; the downtown one is the most convenient.
  • Mod shops — one in Vice City, one near the airport, one in Port Gellhorn.
  • Safehouses — unlock through story; keep at least one on each side of the map.
  • Airport (Escobar International) — flights, vehicle storage and several missions.
  • The port — industrial area, useful for losing pursuers among the containers.

The map rewards exploration: drive every road once, mark what you find, and Leonida starts to feel like home.
